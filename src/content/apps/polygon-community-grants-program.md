## Overview

The **Polygon Community Grants Program** is a large-scale ecosystem funding initiative operated by Polygon Labs to accelerate development across the Polygon ecosystem. The program supports builders, startups, infrastructure teams, and public goods contributors creating applications or tools that expand Polygon’s utility and adoption.

The initiative is designed to fund both early-stage experimentation and mature, production-ready solutions.

---

## What It Funds

The program supports a broad range of categories, including:

- Core infrastructure & protocol tooling  
- Developer tools & SDKs  
- DeFi protocols  
- Gaming & consumer applications  
- AI-integrated applications  
- Public goods & ecosystem services  
- Community-led initiatives  

Projects may apply under structured grant tracks or through direct grant allocations.

---

## Grant Structure

The program distributes funding from a long-term allocation of **1 billion POL tokens**, with seasonal allocations for active funding rounds. 

Applicants typically submit:

- Project overview & technical details  
- Roadmap & milestones  
- Budget breakdown  
- Team information  
- Expected ecosystem impact  

Reviews are conducted by ecosystem contributors and grant committees depending on the track.

---

## Who Can Apply

The program is open globally to:

- Independent developers  
- Startups  
- DAOs  
- Research teams  
- Public goods contributors  

There are no strict geographic restrictions. Both early-stage prototypes and scaling projects are eligible.

---

## Why It Matters

Polygon has grown into one of the most active Ethereum scaling ecosystems. By providing structured funding pathways, the Community Grants Program lowers the barrier to entry for builders while encouraging long-term ecosystem sustainability.

The initiative emphasizes:

- Ecosystem growth  
- Open innovation  
- Public goods support  
- Developer accessibility  

---

## Application Process

1. Visit the official grants portal: https://polygon.technology/grants/  
2. Select the appropriate grant track  
3. Complete the application form  
4. Undergo review & feedback process  
5. Receive milestone-based funding (if approved)  

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis during active seasons.
