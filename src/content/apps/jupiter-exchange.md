### Overview

Jupiter Exchange is the leading liquidity aggregator on Solana. It routes token swaps across multiple decentralized exchanges to ensure optimal pricing and minimal slippage.

By integrating liquidity from Raydium, Orca, Meteora, and other Solana-native DEXs, Jupiter provides efficient routing and real-time optimization.

### Problem

Liquidity fragmentation across Solana DEXs creates inefficiencies. Traders must compare prices manually, leading to slippage and missed opportunities.

---

### Solution

Jupiter aggregates liquidity and automatically routes swaps through the most efficient execution path across integrated decentralized exchanges.
